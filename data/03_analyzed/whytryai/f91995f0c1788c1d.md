Last week, I had AI models generating images.

This week, I flipped the script: Can AI models “see” images?

I put five free-tier chatbots through four vision challenges

If you’ve ever wondered whether your chatbot of choice can actually understand what you show it, this one should give you a clear answer.

(Spoiler alert: only one model found Waldo, and it’s one I didn’t expect.)
