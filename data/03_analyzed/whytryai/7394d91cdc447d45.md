# Here Are My Go-To AI Tools

### My preferred LLMs, image and video models, music makers, research tools, and more.

**LATEST UPDATE: July 8, 2026**

*I first published this round-up on October 17, 2024. But things in AI move fast, so I release regular updates. Check out this archive page with the past versions (fun for tracing how AI and my preferences evolved).*

I often get asked about AI tools that I personally use.

While I regularly test and review all sorts of AI models and sites, most don’t end up a part of my routine.

Here are those that did.

## 🗣️ 1. Large language models

These are the chatbots and LLMs I turn to.

**🎁Free:****Claude Sonnet 5**[**UPDATED - replaced 4.6**] for most creative work. Claude has a “fresh” conversational feel and tends to suggest unconventional ideas. Interactive explainers are awesome for visualizing and grasping tricky concepts. The only downside is the strict message limit for free accounts.**GPT 5.5 Instant**[**UPDATED - replaced GPT 5.4**]holds up nicely for regular day-to-day chats and low-stakes research.

**💵Paid:****Claude 4.6 Opus**is my primary daily driver for everything I do with Claude Code. It runs most of my projects and tasks. (I’m still hesitant to switch to Opus 4.8 due to higher token use and negative public consensus.)**GPT 5.5 High [NEW]**is my go-to for most heavy research. It tends to do a solid job of surfacing and validating sources, then returning a vetted answer.


## 🖼️ 2. AI images

Here are my favorite image models.

**🎁Free**:**Gemini 3.1 Flash Image**(aka**Nano Banana 2**)for image editing. Excellent at detail-preserving image edits from natural-language requests.**GPT Image 2**: for leaderboard-topping image generation. It understands context and can render entire pages of text within an image. (I made a swipe file for it with 100 use cases and prompts.)

**💵Paid**:**Midjourney**: I’ve written dozens of posts about Midjourney over the years. I have honestly not used it much lately, but it remains excellent for artistic exploration and photographic images.


## 📽️ 3. AI video

The AI video space is making the biggest leaps lately. Here’s my take on AI video models based on thorough tests of both text-to-video and image-to-video.

**🎁Free:****Gemini Omni Flash [NEW - replacing Veo 3.1]**is a top-ranked model for context-aware videos with native audio. It’s also great for natural-language video edits. The Flow platform gives you free daily credits to try it.

**💵 Paid:****Seedance 2.0**: Still the #1 video model (but Seedance 2.5 is coming) if you want the cutting-edge stuff. (I get to use it on Genspark with my credits.)
