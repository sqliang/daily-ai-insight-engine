### Report an Emergency

Call 911 for police, fire, or medical emergencies that require an immediate response, including situations where lives are in danger or a crime is in progress.

### Report Non-Emergencies

If you are reporting a non-emergency, such as the examples below, see your options to report crime.

a serious crime with a lengthy time delay, such as an assault the previous night

lost or stolen property under $25,000

lost or stolen property with no suspect

car break-in

vandalism to property or vehicle where repairs expected be under $25,000

noisy party


You can also find alternate resources in case your issue is not a police matter.
