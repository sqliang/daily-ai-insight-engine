  of every US courseGoogle filters for golf course results are terrible so I built
  a better way to browse courses using OSM as the backbone.... https://golfcoursebrowser.com/It''s
  a work in progress and mostly US for now, but I want to expand to the rest of North
  America, the EU, and the rest of the world ASAP.It''s free, no ads, no login, no
  bs. If you spot anything wrong (bad info, a missing course, wrong scorecard), you
  can flag it right on the course page. I actually read those and fix them.Still a
  lot of missing info, but I''m filling out more and more each day. The goal is the
  most complete, and current golf course directory in the world, verified and maintained
  by actual golfers. I think is attainable in the next few months. The base layer
  is OpenStreetMap, cross-checked against course websites and enriched with scorecards,
  USGA rating/slope, and public/private status. User corrections from golfers who
