It makes me so happy when I find out there's a new 8-Bit guy video up, thank you for all the effort and creativity you put in to make them for us David!

I actually did this back in the day on an Amiga to drive 3 monochrome monitors using analog RGB and I wrote some simple games for that setup. Note that the planar graphics of the Amiga makes writing software much easier. If you use the right color palette every monitor gets its own bitplane so independent output on each monitor becomes easy. You can even create fast animation on each monitor using the blitter. Since the Amiga supports 32 independent colors you can use up to 5 bitplanes, allowing for either one black and white image and two images with 4 levels of grey or two black and white images with an additional image with 8 levels of grey.
