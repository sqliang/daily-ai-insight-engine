**HTMX 4.0, the first JavaScript library to release exclusively on the Game Boy® platform!**

Experience the power of HTMX, now on your favorite portable handheld.

Four levels of pickle-collecting excitement. Squish client-side JS down to size, avoid slop and, if you have the skills, defeat Warren to unlock the htmx 4.0 source code!

Be the hero that the web needs!
