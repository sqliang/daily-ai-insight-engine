Community + Open Maps

### Offline Search and Route

Plan and navigate your trip abroad with just GPS, no need for mobile data. Search waypoints while on distant hiking trails or bike paths.

### No Data Collection

The app is designed with privacy in mind - does not identify people, does not track you, and does not collect any information. CoMaps was also audited by

### Save Your Battery

Efficiently uses the battery, doesn’t drain your battery like other navigation apps.

### Free and Built by the Community

People like you are helping build the app by adding locations to

, giving feedback on features, and contributing code on to create great maps together. The project is a fork of Organic Maps and Maps.Me, and driven by an open-source community.
