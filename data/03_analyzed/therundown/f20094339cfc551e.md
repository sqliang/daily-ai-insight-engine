**The Rundown:**Apple just [filed](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28453229-apple-v-openai/?utm_campaign=apple-takes-openai-to-court&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) a lawsuit against OpenAI, its hardware chief Tang Tan, and its io devices unit, alleging they turned an Apple poaching spree into a pipeline for confidential hardware theft while calling for a redesign of the lab’s unreleased device.

**The details:**

*   Apple says it flagged the suspected theft in a February letter and heard nothing back, and that more than 400 of its former employees now work at the lab.

*   Hardware chief Tang Tan, who joined OAI after 24 years at Apple, allegedly ran interviews where candidates were told to come with 'actual parts'.

*   Ex-iPhone engineer Chang Liu is accused of using a bug to gain confidential files after joining OAI, [texting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-11/openai-engineer-s-lol-moment-set-stage-for-legal-fight-with-apple?utm_campaign=apple-takes-openai-to-court&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) a colleague that the access was 'so funny.'
