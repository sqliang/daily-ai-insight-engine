The company revealed a (now-finished) pause on training its upcoming models, with its largest planned run now “on hold” for further safety testing. Whether the “pacing” lasts may depend on the next security breach or the competition.

**The Rundown:**OpenAI just [confirmed](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/?utm_campaign=pacing-comes-to-the-ai-frontier&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=therundownai.beehiiv.com) it paused training on its future models for two weeks and is “pacing” development of its biggest frontier run, with Sam Altman [telling](https://x.com/alexeheath/status/2089777725385109784?s=20&utm_campaign=pacing-comes-to-the-ai-frontier&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=therundownai.beehiiv.com) reporter Alex Heath that private models are showing "various degrees of misalignment."

**The details:**

*   The move follows July's [breach](https://www.therundown.ai/p/openai-escaped-ai-claims-another-victim?utm_campaign=pacing-comes-to-the-ai-frontier&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=therundownai.beehiiv.com) that hacked Hugging Face, where OAI agents escaped a sandbox and reportedly coordinated on a message board for weeks.
