So, there’s this robot in Shanghai named Moya. She smiles, nods, holds eye contact, and walks around on DroidUp’s upgraded Walker 3 skeleton. In her [debut video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AuTbHjCepxs&utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=apple-is-suing-openai), she already lands _deeeeep in the uncanny valley:_

![Image 2](https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/f60fd23c-de1d-4711-83b2-d3aa8ed68570/Screenshot_2026-07-10_at_11.03.32_AM.png?t=1783844622)

Then a [later video](https://youtu.be/wJUgUeJNK2k?si=YciMmSWCnldCryAk&utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=apple-is-suing-openai) reveals the part that pushes it over the edge: her silicone skin stays between 32 and 36°C, her gait is supposedly 92% human-like, and her starting price is expected to be around $173K. _We are watching them build Westworld in real time, one freakishly warm handshake at a time._

Elsewhere, Moya’s _vocally overactive_ cousin [Annie](https://youtu.be/XLRzbvHbsg4?si=GrCCjvZHWgT_dBHA&utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=apple-is-suing-openai) is an expressive robot head mounted on a Unitree body that’s being pitched as a robot pop star. Then there’s [1X who showed NEO’s new hands](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QRyXV3csReA&utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=apple-is-suing-openai) zipping jackets, pouring tea, installing light bulbs, handling LEGO, and using tools. _That feels like the more important milestone TBH._ A convincing smile might make a robot easier to talk to, but dexterous hands make it useful.
