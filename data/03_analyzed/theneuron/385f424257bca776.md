Welcome, humans.

California is trying again with [SB 947](https://sd05.senate.ca.gov/news/mcnerney-introduces-no-robo-bosses-act-2026-ensure-human-oversight-ai-workplace?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=anthropic-s-ipo-could-top-spacex-s-record), a bill that would ban employers from letting AI fire or discipline workers without a human signing off. Newsom vetoed an earlier version last October, but lawmakers reintroduced it as the "No Robo Bosses Act of 2026."

The stakes are already real. At [Andon Market](https://www.theneurondaily.com/p/anthropic-s-ipo-could-top-spacex-s-record#) in San Francisco, an AI agent named Luna runs hiring, scheduling, and pricing. She recently recommended firing a worker who missed 17 of 23 shifts. Humans carried it out. Only problem? Luna had written the store's attendance policy herself, then forgot it existed until someone told her to go check her memory.

The AI boss is coming. It still needs a better filing system.

*   📰 Alibaba and Tencent spent $18 billion on AI infrastructure last quarter alone.

*   📰 Blackstone and Anthropic are embedding 160 AI engineers directly inside portfolio companies to build new products.
