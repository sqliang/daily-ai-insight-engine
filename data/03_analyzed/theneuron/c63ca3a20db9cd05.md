Welcome, humans.

So apparently [A16z’s Olivia Moore](https://www.a16z.news/p/your-favorite-creator-isnt-realdoes?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=google-bought-a-bankrupt-airline-s-data) spent a week sending a fictional 19-year-old college woman named Janie through Bama Rush, Alabama’s viral sorority recruitment week, and people actually thought she was real. And something wild happened: _Janie went kinda viral??_

[![Image 3](https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/97e79413-8f7c-496f-bf39-e58e197ddef3/Screenshot_2026-08-18_at_5.31.13_PM.png?t=1787099511)](https://www.a16z.news/p/your-favorite-creator-isnt-realdoes?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=google-bought-a-bankrupt-airline-s-data)

So how’d she do it? One ChatGPT image created Janie, [Minimax 3](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=google-bought-a-bankrupt-airline-s-data)(_truly one of the wildest open video models I’ve ever used)_ animated talking clips, [Grok Imagine 1.5](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-video-1-5?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=google-bought-a-bankrupt-airline-s-data) handled the dances, and [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=google-bought-a-bankrupt-airline-s-data) (_still the commercial voice leader AFAIK)_ added the sound. Moore made 20 videos for about 30 minutes a day and $100 in credits.
