## We explain when to use which, and why

Welcome, humans.

We’re **LIVE right now**** ****(click to join!)** talking all things Skills vs Agents vs GPTs vs Projects vs Gems vs Plugins… *yooou naaaame it, we’re going to try to explaaain it. *

**See, AI has given us a new kind of productivity problem: **five, six, maybe even seven different ways to customize our work with agents, three different words for “workflow,” and one very reasonable question: **Which one am I actually supposed to use, when?**

**Today on Neuron LIVE**, we’re sorting the whole mess: AI Skills, Projects, Gems, Custom GPTs, Agents, plugins, and maybe even “loops,” because apparently the AI industry looked at “agents” and thought, “Could use one more term.”

**The goal is simple:** by the end, you should know when to organize work, when to customize an assistant, when to save a reusable workflow, and when to let an AI system actually go do something.

*This is basically the guide we wish existed before every AI lab dumped all these confusing terms on us. *

## What we’re testing live, on stream:

**Projects**(ChatGPT, Claude): when you need a place to organize ongoing work, context, files, instructions, and repeat tasks.**Gems & Custom GPTs**(Gemini Gems, Custom GPTs): when you want a reusable custom assistant for a specific job.**Skills**(OpenAI, Codex, Claude, Gemini ADK): when you want repeatable instructions, workflows, references, scripts, and process memory the AI can load when needed.**Agents**(ChatGPT Agent, OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude Code agents, Google ADK): when you want AI to take actions on your behalf autonomously towards a goal.**Plugins**(Codex plugins, ChatGPT Apps SDK, Claude plugins, Google ADK plugins): because yes, those are somehow back in the conversation too.**Loops**(Addy Osmani’s loop engineering post, Claude dynamic workflows, Google ADK loop workflows): if we have time, we’ll touch the trendiest topic in AI discourse without making everyone pretend they already know what it means.
