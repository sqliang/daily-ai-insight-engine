Welcome, humans!

_Getting the dang thing into_ _**TestFlight and the Apple App Store**_ _._

Building an app with AI is getting easier. Getting that app through Apple’s doors is still a, call it _different_ adventure.

So today, Grant and Corey are showing the beginner version of the process, from App Store Connect to loading screenshots to privacy questions to the moment you finally submit the build and wait for Apple’s review.

[![Image 2: The Neuron live stream thumbnail for How to Publish Your AI-Built App to the App Store](https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/a999e120-650f-4d83-b44a-784c92836829/LIVE_Thumbnails__YT___LI___7_.png?t=1785352461)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7xo46iPvoc&utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=live-publish-your-ai-built-app-to-the-app-store)

## 🔴 What we’ll cover live

*   **App Store Connect:** what you need to set up before Apple will let your app anywhere near users.

*   **TestFlight:** how to prepare your AI-built app for beta testing before the public App Store listing.

*   **Store assets:** screenshots, descriptions, icons, and the other bits that make “ship it” become admin work.

*   **Privacy questions:** the Apple review details that can slow you down if you discover them too late.

*   **The review process:** what confused us, what delayed us, and what we’d do differently next time.

The goal is simple: help you understand what happens after the prototype works, especially if you used AI coding tools to build something real and now want people to test it.

Bring your questions, especially the basic ones. The App Store process has a way of making everyone feel like they missed a secret orientation meeting.
