# Inside the research: The state of AI transformation

In a new global study, Notion surveyed 6,000+ professionals across 10 markets to understand where organizations really are in their AI transformations. The results show the conversation has outrun reality: most organizations are just starting, leaders and workers experience adoption differently, and the biggest unlock isn’t more tools—it’s trust, governance, and workflow integration.

Join Notion for a deep dive into the findings, followed by a conversation with experts on what it takes to translate insight into real-world workflow change.

## What you’ll learn

- Where AI adoption really is today (and why “everyone else is ahead” is usually the wrong benchmark)
- The biggest perception gap: how leaders vs. frontline teams experience AI adoption differently
- The conditions for scaling AI responsibly: trust + visibility + governance
