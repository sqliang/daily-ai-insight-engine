# Title:AI Learning and Conceptual Transfer in the Game of Hidden Rules

View PDF HTML (experimental)Abstract:This report summarizes the work conducted on the Game of Hidden Rules (GOHR), focusing on reinforcement learning agents trained to infer hidden rules from trial-and-error feedback, representation design, rule difficulty analysis, transfer learning, generalization, and pseudo-bot-assisted human learning analysis. The report focuses on the Transformer-based A2C framework, Feature-Centric and Object-Centric representations, experimental findings, and classification of human learning data.
