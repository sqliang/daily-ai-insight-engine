Description: Omni is the AI engineer that takes your agents from a laptop to the cloud. Describe what you want, or bring what you've built, and Omni wires the tools and skills, tests on mock data, and hands you a running cloud agent: scheduled, long-running, shareable with your team. It keeps agents healthy too - improves system prompts, compares models, debugs and fixes failed runs.
