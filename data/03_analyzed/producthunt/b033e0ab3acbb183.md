# CodeBurn

Product Hunt product page for CodeBurn.

Tagline: See where your AI coding spend actually goes

Description: CodeBurn is a free, open-source tracker for AI coding costs. It reads the session files your tools already write - Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot, 40 in all, and shows every token and dollar by the task, model, project, and pull request that used it. The Optimize tab finds waste like cache bloat or retry tax, applies the fix, and tracks what it actually saved. Everything runs on your machine: no account, no uploads. MIT-licensed and genuinely free, used by 150k+ developers across the world.
