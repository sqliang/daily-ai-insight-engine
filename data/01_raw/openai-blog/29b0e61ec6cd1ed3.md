We’re bringing OpenAI’s frontier cyber models to the security partners protecting organizations around the world. Together, we’re putting frontier intelligence directly into the products, services, and security operations defenders already depend on. More organizations can now find serious vulnerabilities, fix them faster, and stay ahead of threats moving at machine speed.

AI is changing cybersecurity faster than most organizations can respond. Attackers can identify vulnerabilities, develop exploits, and move through complex systems with increasing speed and scale. At the same time, security teams are confronting a growing volume of weaknesses across the software and infrastructure they protect. Finding those vulnerabilities is only the beginning. The harder challenge is determining which ones pose a real threat and fixing them before they can be exploited. Too many defenders still lack access to the frontier models that can help them do that.

These partners bring deep security expertise and established relationships with organizations around the world. By bringing our frontier cyber models into their services, we can help more defenders find serious vulnerabilities, validate which ones matter, and fix them faster.

A vulnerability report does not protect an organization. Protection comes from understanding whether a weakness can actually be exploited, identifying the systems at risk, developing a fix, and getting that fix into production.

Daybreak Cyber Partners bring OpenAI’s most advanced cyber models into the work they already do to protect their customers. They know their customers’ systems, understand how their security teams operate, and can help determine which vulnerabilities matter and how to fix them.

Depending on the engagement, partners can help with vulnerability discovery and validation, red teaming, penetration testing, incident response, and remediation across complex enterprise systems.

Most organizations want advanced security capabilities that fit into how their teams already work, integrating with the platforms, providers, and workflows they rely on to protect their business.
