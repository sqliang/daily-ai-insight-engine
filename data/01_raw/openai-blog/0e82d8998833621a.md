Virgin Atlantic is using ChatGPT Work to help its teams understand the airline’s complex customer journey and deliver better experiences faster. From competitive research to product planning and analytics, ChatGPT Work gives employees new ways to bring information together and move from insight to action.

For an airline, the customer journey extends beyond just booking a flight. It includes browsing, purchasing, checking in, traveling, the onboard experience, and sharing feedback afterward. Each stage produces valuable information, often held across different systems and reporting tools. Virgin Atlantic is using ChatGPT to connect those signals and help teams decide where to focus.

As Virgin Atlantic developed its new five-year strategy, its digital product team needed a clear view of the competitive landscape. Traditionally, assessing multiple competitors across a detailed customer journey could take weeks of manual research.

Using ChatGPT Work, the team created a structured framework and asked it to research competitor experiences, trace available customer journeys, and organize its findings into a dataset for review. The team could start the work in the morning, return a few hours later, and begin evaluating and refining the results.

“As part of Virgin Atlantic’s five-year digital strategy, ChatGPT Work has helped us explore customer journeys and market experiences in a more structured and efficient way,” says Nathan Bolt, Head of Digital Product at Virgin Atlantic. “It enables our teams to compare journey patterns, surface opportunities, and turn insight into product decisions far faster than before. What might once have taken weeks can now be accelerated into hours, helping us focus investment where it can make the biggest difference for our customers.”

The analysis helped Virgin Atlantic identify areas where its digital experience was performing strongly, capabilities it needed to protect, and opportunities that warranted further investment. Those findings informed recommendations for the company’s five-year plan.

ChatGPT Work also helps the team synthesize strategy documents, customer insights, and digital experience data, making that information easier to explore and apply throughout the product lifecycle. “ChatGPT has allowed us to go from idea to actual product in customers’ hands much faster,” Bolt says.

Virgin Atlantic’s customer experience and analytics teams face another challenge: bringing together data from many sources. Previously, employees had to navigate several dashboard tools, authenticated workspaces, licenses, user profiles, and report suites to understand different parts of the journey.

With ChatGPT Work and ChatGPT Sites, Virgin Atlantic is creating secure, authenticated dashboards that bring disparate datasets into a common interface. Employees across the business can access relevant views, explore information more easily, and make decisions for their own areas.
