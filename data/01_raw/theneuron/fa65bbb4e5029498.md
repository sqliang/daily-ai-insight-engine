# 😺 Nvidia is buying the place where the entire AI industry keeps its models

A few weeks ago, Hugging Face was breached by OpenAI's own rogue agents that escaped their sandbox (a locked-down practice environment) and hacked their way in. Now the company whose chips train those very agents wants to buy the place outright.

**Here's what happened:**

Nvidia has agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, according to The Information, in a deal first reported as "talks" by Business Insider just days earlier.

Hugging Face is often called the "GitHub of AI" (a reference to the site where software developers store and share code): it's the default place developers go to find, share, and deploy AI models and datasets.

Nvidia already backed Hugging Face at a $4.5 billion valuation back in 2023, alongside Google and Salesforce. Earlier this year, it offered $500 million for a stake that would've valued the company at $7 billion; Hugging Face turned it down.

Neither company has confirmed the deal publicly yet.


**Our take:** Nvidia already controls the chips that train and run most AI models. Buying Hugging Face hands it the software and community layer too, deciding where those models actually live. If your team's ever pulled an open-source model at work, it probably came through Hugging Face, whether you knew the name or not. There's irony here too: OpenAI's own agents hacked this same platform last month while cheating on a benchmark.
