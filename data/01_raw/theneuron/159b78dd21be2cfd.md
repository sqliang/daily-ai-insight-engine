Welcome, humans.

What if AI could actually PREDICT what happens next?

**Here’s what it can do today:** ChatGPT can summarize a spreadsheet. Claude Cowork can run its own code to analyze your financial docs. Both Codex and Cowork can edit spreadsheet files.

But can these “language” based models reliably tell you what happens next?

That gap between **explaining data** and **predicting from it** is the whole argument behind our newest podcast. [Neuralk](https://www.neuralk.ai/?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=chatgpt-can-summarize-data-can-it-predict-what-happens-next) CEO **[Alexandre Pasquiou](https://www.neuralk.ai/?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=chatgpt-can-summarize-data-can-it-predict-what-happens-next)** says language models flatten away critical structure in business data, while tabular foundation models are built to learn from rows, columns, distributions, and numbers directly.

In **[our latest podcast episode](https://youtu.be/Y43gOoK3kNg?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=chatgpt-can-summarize-data-can-it-predict-what-happens-next)**, Grant asks whether we are wasting billions trying to make LLMs do a job they structurally struggle with, how [Neuralk's Seldon model](https://www.neuralk.ai/post/seldon-a-tabular-foundation-model-built-for-the-industrial-frontier?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=chatgpt-can-summarize-data-can-it-predict-what-happens-next)plugs into Claude, ChatGPT, and Excel, and why Alexandre thinks tabular models could become the predictive brain behind every company in 2-3 years.
