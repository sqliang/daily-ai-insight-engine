Welcome, humans.

What if every hospital could give patients access to the world’s best surgeon, performing at their absolute best, every single day?

That is the long-term promise of autonomous surgery. But before you picture a robot rolling into the operating room alone, [our latest podcast episode](https://youtu.be/yiQDaKIsZQE?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=watch-would-you-trust-an-ai-surgeon) explains what the technology can do today, what remains science fiction, and why progress looks more like a ladder than a giant leap.

[![Image 3](https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/9652e77b-46ad-48ee-b4a3-cc7c4987a411/TN_Thumbnail_MathiasUnberath.png?t=1784741608)](https://youtu.be/yiQDaKIsZQE?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=watch-would-you-trust-an-ai-surgeon)

Grant sat down with **Mathias Unberath**, CTO and co-founder of **Inner Logic**, formerly Semaphor, and an award-winning computer vision and simulation researcher at Johns Hopkins Whiting School of Engineering. Inner Logic just announced an **$11.5M seed round** co-led by General Catalyst and Bison Ventures to build the simulation and validation infrastructure surgical autonomy will need before it reaches patients.

_No pressure. It only needs to work while someone is open on the table._
