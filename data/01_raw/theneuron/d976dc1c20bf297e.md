Welcome, humans.

Ryan Singer had a very good complaint about Jira, Linear, and every other work tracker: they do not show the budget behind the work. Who authorized this task? What capacity is it spending? What did we choose not to do instead?

That feels like the next useful AI benchmark: can the model estimate the token budget required to complete a task, translate that into cost, and map it to rough real-world time? Basically, can it understand its own token burn rate per hour? Forget “can it pass the test.” Show me the AI that can say, “This will cost about $14.80 and three human-equivalent hours.” Now _that_ is AGI.

**Here's what happened in AI today:**

*   😼 Google released Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber, splitting cheaper agent workflows from restricted cyber-defense use.

*   📰 OpenAI turned ChatGPT into a full business platform with ads, small-business agents, and a 3.2 GW Georgia data center.

*   📰 OpenAI and Anthropic lobbied Washington to restrict Chinese open-weight AI models.

*   📰 Nvidia's Jensen Huang pushed back, arguing those models aren't a threat worth blocking.
