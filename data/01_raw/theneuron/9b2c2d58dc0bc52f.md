Welcome, humans.

**Entrepreneurs and builders, this one’s for you.**

AI can now help a nontechnical founder turn an idea into a working prototype before bed.

The uncomfortable part comes the next morning, when that prototype needs authentication, security, reliable infrastructure, manageable cloud bills, and enough resilience to survive actual contact with paying customers.

In [**our latest podcast episode**](https://youtu.be/HWxpUDRI0_w?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=aws-put-a-cto-inside-claude-code), Corey and Grant spoke with [**Deap Ubhi**](https://x.com/deapubhi?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=aws-put-a-cto-inside-claude-code), Global Director of Technology for Startups at [**AWS**](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-startups/advisor/?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=aws-put-a-cto-inside-claude-code), about the new dividing line between _making software_ and building something people can safely depend on.

[Video 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HWxpUDRI0_w)

Deap has built several companies himself. His biggest observation about the AI era is simple: **startup iteration cycles have collapsed from months and quarters into hours and days.**

A founder can now build a prototype, hand it to prospective customers, collect feedback, and ship the next version while the original idea is still warm.

_The bad news is that you can also build 15 side projects and accidentally turn your life into a browser tab._

## Here’s our favorite parts:

*   **(**[**05:54**](https://youtu.be/HWxpUDRI0_w?t=354&utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=aws-put-a-cto-inside-claude-code)**) Your startup suddenly gets more lottery tickets:** Faster iteration gives founders many more attempts to discover what customers actually want before their runway disappears.
