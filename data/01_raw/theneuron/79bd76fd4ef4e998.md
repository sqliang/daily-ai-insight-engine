## 😺 Zuckerberg Just Publicly Broke Ranks With OpenAI and Anthropic On Slowing Down AI

Yesterday we told you that [Sam Altman and Dario Amodei](https://www.pacingthefrontier.com/?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=zuckerberg-split-with-his-own-ai-chief) backed a petition asking Washington to build the tools to "pace" AI development. Well, [Mark Zuckerberg](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/mark-zuckerberg-says-u-s-should-accelerate-ai-development-not-restrict-it-3bbe0868?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=zuckerberg-split-with-his-own-ai-chief) took out a Wall Street Journal op-ed to go out on his own and say: hard pass.

**Here's what happened:**

*   Zuckerberg argued the U.S. "should accelerate AI development, not restrict it," and said the real risk isn't AI moving too fast, it's "superintelligence" (AI that outperforms humans at most tasks) ending up locked inside a handful of labs.
