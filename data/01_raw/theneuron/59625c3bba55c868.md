# 😺 Grok 4.6 Is Here, and it’s GPT 5.6 Level?!

Nuff said in the headline, am I right?

**Here’s what happened:**

SpaceXAI released Grok 4.6 for long-running agents, coding, research, and more ambitious interactive work.

Artificial Analysis scored it 61, up five points from Grok 4.5 and level with

*GPT-5.6 Sol overall.*It is available now in Cursor, Grok Build, the API, OpenRouter, Vercel, and Cloudflare, with 2x included usage in Cursor and Grok Build for the first week.


AI agents get expensive fast. A model can look brilliant in one answer and still be a bad deal if a multi-hour task takes twice as many turns, tokens, and retries. Grok 4.6's pitch is that it can stay near the frontier without the frontier bill.

On price, xAI’s current pricing page includes Grok 4.6 on the $30/month SuperGrok plan, while API pricing starts at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. Artificial Analysis (linked above) measured it at $0.84 per task and placed it on its cost-performance frontier across every agentic evaluation in the index.

The efficiency matters A LOT on long jobs. On AA-Briefcase, a Grok reached Fable 5-tier while averaging about 53 turns and 0.5B input tokens, versus roughly 103 turns and 2.0B for Claude Opus 5 Max. Fewer turns and less context can compound into much lower agent costs over hours of work.

**Why this matters:** If Grok can stay near frontier quality while burning fewer turns and cheaper tokens, teams running agents all day definitely care more about cost per completed task, and will more than likely make the switch.
