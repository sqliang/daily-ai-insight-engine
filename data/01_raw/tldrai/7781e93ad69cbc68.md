Today, we are officially introducing SeedRealtime, a native audio-visual full-duplex LLM.

As a key step toward omni-modal interaction, SeedRealtime natively unifies audio, video, and text within a single architecture, enabling real-time interaction over continuous multimodal streams and a new experience of watching, listening, and speaking simultaneously. The model advances audio-visual joint understanding, proactive interaction, and conversational timing.

End-to-end human evaluations show that, compared with cascaded models, SeedRealtime reduces conversational pacing issues by half, significantly decreases interruptions, latency, and false triggers, and also markedly improves the usability rate of single-turn conversations in terms of smoothness and completeness.

Currently, SeedRealtime has been fully rolled out, pioneering large-scale deployment of audio-visual full-duplex technology in the industry.
