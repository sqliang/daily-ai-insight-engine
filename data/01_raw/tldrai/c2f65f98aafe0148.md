# Build strong data foundations for agentic AI at scale


Enterprise AI and data leader virtual panel

Join senior enterprise leaders from Prudential Insurance, Siemens, GAF, HF Sinclair, and Amazon Web Services (AWS) and discover how to build resilient, scalable data foundations for enterprise AI. Learn practical strategies to build modern data architectures and production-ready AI agents, turning your data into a long-term competitive advantage.

Turn governed data products into faster, more informed decision-making at scale.

Plus, see how AWS Marketplace streamlines the way you discover, procure, and deploy data and AI solutions.

## Key takeaways

- Build safe, industrial-scale systems with classical and gen AI.
- Power analytics and AI with high-quality, governed data.
- Choose the right databases to support AI-based applications.
- Streamline solution discovery and deployment with AWS Marketplace.

Generative AI holds immense potential for transforming security operations. However, organizations need practical guidance to navigate considerations around data governance, model integrity, and responsible AI adoption. Additionally, organizations require a phased approach to confidently explore use cases, build proof of concepts (POCs), and scale to production deployments.

In this webinar, experts from SANS Institute and Amazon Web Services (AWS) provide a strategic roadmap for securely harnessing generative AI for development and data governance with Amazon Bedrock. You’ll examine key ethical considerations and technical challenges, experience the adoption lifecycle, and explore real-world implementations.
