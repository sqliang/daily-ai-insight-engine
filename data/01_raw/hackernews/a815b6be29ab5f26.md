# putt.day

A daily hole of mini golf.

No. 32 · June 13, 2026

## How to play

- Grab the ball, drag back, let go. Pull further to hit harder.
- Drag anywhere else to look around; pinch or scroll to zoom.
- Get it in the hole in as few strokes as you can.
- Water sends you back to where you were.
- One new hole a day. Your first finish counts. Old days are in the calendar.
