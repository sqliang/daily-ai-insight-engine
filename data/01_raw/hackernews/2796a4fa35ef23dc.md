WHY UNA EXISTS

## When one part fails,  the whole watch shouldn't.

Most smartwatches are sealed products. When the battery fades, a sensor fails, or support ends, the entire device is often replaced.

UNA is designed around a different idea: a GPS Watch you can repair, upgrade and keep.

![Image 2](https://unawatch.com/cdn/shop/files/DSC05249.jpg?v=1783695266&width=1600)

## A New Era

## Of Battery

UNA features a 280 mAh hard-shell battery that can be replaced with a single screwdriver. This battery is the first of its kind in wearables.

![Image 3](https://unawatch.com/cdn/shop/files/TL_-_2_Watches_Air_3_-_Edited_818bf33e-30b7-4d1a-9697-caedaebcff7f.png?v=1783937263&width=1200)

## Charges With

## USB Type-C

Gone are the days of brand specific charging cables. UNA features a universal USB-C charging cable, the same as your phone.

![Image 4](https://unawatch.com/cdn/shop/files/Scroll_Features_-_Repair_Upgrade.png?v=1783706425&width=600)

### REPAIRABLE & UPGRADEABLE

The first wearable of its kind to give users easy access to all of the watch internals. This means replacing a cracked screen or a dead battery is easier than ever.

![Image 5](https://unawatch.com/cdn/shop/files/Scroll_Features_-_Run_ride_and_hike.png?v=1783706425&width=600)

### TRACK YOUR RUNS, RIDES & HIKES

UNA keeps things simple, with a clear focus on tracking your activities without the fluff. This means more adventures and less frustration.

![Image 6](https://unawatch.com/cdn/shop/files/Scroll_Features_-_Strava.png?v=1783706425&width=600)

### SEAMLESSLY SYNCS TO STRAVA

Once you've completed your activity, simply connect your watch to the UNA mobile app and your activity will quickly sync to Strava.

![Image 7](https://unawatch.com/cdn/shop/files/Scroll_Features_-_Dev_Platform.png?v=1783706424&width=600)

### OPEN DEVELOPER PLATFORM

UNA gives developers access to the tools, data and sensors needed to build custom apps and experiences.

![Image 8](https://unawatch.com/cdn/shop/files/App.png?v=1784045630&width=600)

### UNA MOBILE APP

UNA pairs with easy-to-use application available on both iOS & Android that unlocks even more features.
