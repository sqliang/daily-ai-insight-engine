**The Rundown:**Over 1,000 staffers from AI labs, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, just [signed](https://www.pacingthefrontier.com/?utm_campaign=1-000-frontier-staffers-ask-for-an-ai-brake-pedal&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) a ‘Pacing the Frontier’ letter, calling on the U.S. to help build tools that could “deliberately pace” AI progress as the world nears autonomous AI research.

**The details:**

*   The list covers nearly a dozen labs, including Anthropic co-founders Jack Clark and Chris Olah and chief scientists from OAI, Meta, and Thinking Machines.

*   The statement does not call for a pause, but instead asks for an international effort to give governments and labs the choice to slow down if needed.

*   The letter pins the risk on automated AI research, warning models that improve other models could accelerate "beyond our ability to understand or control."
