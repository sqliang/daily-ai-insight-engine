**The Rundown:**Anthropic just [ended](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2078302415804379218?s=20&utm_campaign=anthropic-s-fable-survives-the-subscription-axe&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) a month of shifting deadlines for Claude Fable 5, saying the model will stay inside Max and Team Premium at half of each plan's usage caps, with lower plans getting a one-time $100 credit before moving to pay-per-use.

**The details:**

*   Anthropic had planned to remove Fable from subscriptions completely on a temporary basis, but postponed the cutoff three times in five weeks.

*   Anthropic [called](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2078302415804379218?s=20&utm_campaign=anthropic-s-fable-survives-the-subscription-axe&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) demand “challenging to predict,” admitting the “frustrating” usage situation and saying it is investing in more compute for improved access.
