Apple bumped up its trade-in offers for iPhones, iPads, Macs, Apple Watches, and certain Android phones, with some devices now worth over $100 more, *9to5Mac* reports. The Mac Studio’s trade-in value increased the most, going up by $260. While some devices’ offers are unchanged, most got an increase of around $5 to $20, with a few seeing an increase of over $100:

# Apple increases trade-in offers and adds new Android devices

New Android trade-ins include devices from Samsung, Google, and OnePlus.

New Android trade-ins include devices from Samsung, Google, and OnePlus.

- MacBook Pro ($855, previously $690)
- Mac Pro ($2,195, previously $2,045)
- Mac Studio ($1,305, previously $1,045)

Additionally, Apple is now offering trade-in credit for a handful of new Android devices:

- Samsung Galaxy S21 Ultra 5G ($95)
- Google Pixel 9 Pro XL ($315)
- Google Pixel 9 Pro ($305)
- Google Pixel 9 ($210)
- OnePlus 13 ($250)
- OnePlus 13R ($165)

While some new Android phones have been added to the list, Apple lowered its trade-in credit for a few others, including:

- Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra 5G ($125, previously $130)
- Google Pixel 8 Pro ($155, previously $165)
- Google Pixel 7 Pro ($90, was $95)
- Google Pixel 7 ($60, was $65)

The higher trade-in credits on most devices follow a wave of price hikes Apple announced earlier this year, with increases ranging from $30 to $4,200. Apple also launched a new “Upgrade” leasing program last month aimed at making its increasingly-pricey devices easier to afford.
