# Cozora’s Summary & Overview

**Image-to-Video AI: Hands-On Masterclass (Daniel Nest)**

Most people have seen AI-generated videos online, and most of what they’ve seen is slop. That reputation is both earned and misleading. It’s earned because the easiest thing to do with these tools *is* to produce something low-effort and forgettable. It’s misleading because the tools themselves are far more capable than their worst output suggests.

This session was a deep dive into the practical, purposeful side of image-to-video AI —the side that doesn’t get much attention. My guest, Daniel Nest, is the creator of *Why Try AI* (16,000+ subscribers), a passionate explorer who has been hands-on with these tools since the early image model days of 2022. He came with demos, frameworks, and a genuine nerd’s enthusiasm for showing what’s actually possible when you approach these tools thoughtfully.

Whether you’re a content creator, marketer, writer, or filmmaker, the techniques Daniel shared have real-world applications. And the core insight running through all of it is deceptively simple: the smarter you are about *what* you give the model, the less work you have to do — and the better the results.

If you weren’t there, the replay is worth your time.

**During the class, we:**

Toured the image-to-video tool landscape, from beginner to advanced

Learned why Google Flow is the best starting point for newcomers

Explored the 5S Framework for building effective video prompts

Saw how image references can replace complex text descriptions

Demonstrated the “starting frame” technique for precise scene control

Used first and last frames to let the model fill the narrative gap

Discovered how annotations and scribbles direct complex on-screen action

Explored character and object reference for consistent brand storytelling

Learned multi-panel storyboarding to generate narrative-driven clips

Discussed where image-to-video AI is heading — and its real ceiling
