Hey folks,

**We have a good Grok model - Grok 4.5**. SpaceXAI and Cursor trained it together, and it’s an Opus-class model. It should perform roughly in between Opus 4.7 and 4.8. But it’s much cheaper on a per-token basis and token-efficient—making it 6x cheaper than Opus models and 3x cheaper than GPT-5.5.
