**Claude Opus 4.8**- Simon Willison calls it a modest but useful upgrade, mostly because it’s more honest about uncertainty and less likely to miss flaws in its own code. Every’s vibe check is more bullish: they found it a big jump from 4.7, strong at coding/writing/knowledge work, and competitive with GPT-5.5 on their internal senior-engineer benchmark.
