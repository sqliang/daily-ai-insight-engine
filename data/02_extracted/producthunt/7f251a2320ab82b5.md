Hi everyone!

Your agent has 60 minutes to ship its work before you [claim it](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/claim-deployments/).

[@Cloudflare](https://www.producthunt.com/products/cloudflare) now has temporary accounts for AI agents. With _wrangler deploy --temporary_, an agent can deploy a real Worker without asking you to sign up, complete OAuth, create an API token, or click through the dashboard.

It gets a temporary preview account, a live workers.dev URL, and a claim link.

The nice part is that the agent can loop during the 60-min window: deploy, test the URL, change the code, redeploy, and verify again.

When the work is useful, you keep it. When it is not, it disappears💨
