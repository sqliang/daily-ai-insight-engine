2026 is shaping up to be an excellent year for Apple TV. Apple’s streaming service has built out an impressive slate that spans returning favorites like *Silo* and *Sugar* to all-new hits including OnlyFans-inspired dramedies, terrifying comedies, and paranoid tech thrillers. But the most important release might be a feel-good sports sitcom. After what seemed like a definitive finale, *Ted Lasso* is back with a fourth season that doubles as a soft reboot for the show, mixing up familiar characters with a substantially new cast. And as Apple TV continues its push for a bigger piece of the streaming market, the return of its most culturally impactful series could go a long way toward solidifying its place against more established competitors.

# Ted Lasso returns at an important time for Apple TV

The end of season 3, which happened in 2023, promised a lot of change and what seemed like a happy ending for much of the main cast. Among many other things, Ted (Jason Sudeikis) was heading back to the US to spend time with his son, Roy (Brett Goldstein) had just been promoted to head coach of AFC Richmond, Coach Beard (Brendan Hunt) got married at Stonehenge, and Richmond’s leadership group of Rebecca (Hannah Waddingham), Keeley (Juno Temple), and Higgins (Jeremy Swift) were flirting with the idea of launching a women’s team. It had the energy of a sitcom ending, and the episode was even called “So Long, Farewell.” But it’s that fledgling women’s team that spurs the show’s return.

The season 4 premiere almost feels like a different show at first, and that largely comes down to location. At the outset, Ted is working part-time at a grocery store in order to have more time to parent, and Rebecca, Keeley, and Higgins have come to Kansas City in an attempt to convince him to be the inaugural coach of the new team. It’s like a reunion episode of sorts, with old friends catching up for the first time in years, which helps viewers understand what has happened in that time. It also drags out Ted’s inevitable decision to say “yes” across a full episode.
