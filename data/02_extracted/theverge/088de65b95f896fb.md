The new Designed for Xbox 25th Anniversary collection features a handful of limited edition gadgets available for pre-order today. Paying homage to the *Halo* special edition of the original Xbox, almost everything in the collection is translucent “OG Green,” matching the Xbox Series X25 limited edition console it announced in June at E3. That includes 25th anniversary versions of the Razer Basilisk V3 Pro 35K mouse, Razer BlackWidow V4 75% gaming keyboard, and Razer Hammerhead V3 X HyperSpeed earbuds.

# Take a look at Microsoft’s new 25th anniversary Halo accessories

Many of the devices feature the original green of Xbox’s special edition *Halo* console.

There’s also a translucent green 8BitDo Ultimate charging dock for Xbox Series X/S and Xbox One controllers, along with a translucent green GameSir X5 Lite for Xbox mobile controller.

Besides a black PowerA sling bag, the only thing in the collection that *isn’t* translucent green is a special edition of the Backbone Pro mobile controller. Following a translucent green version released last year, this edition comes in clear, translucent blue, and translucent orange. It’s a blind box, though, so you could end up with any of the three colors.
