You don’t need a keynote session to make noise at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026. Sometimes the best way to meet investors, customers, and partners is by exhibiting directly on the Expo Hall floor at San Francisco’s Moscone West from October 13-15.

That’s exactly what our Exhibit Program offers. Showcase your startup and get the added bonus of additional tickets for Disrupt to bring even more members of your team and make the most of this exciting opportunity.

## All the perks of Disrupt’s Exhibit Tables

Our exhibit tables start at $12,500, and when you break down all the included benefits, the returns speak for themselves. Here’s what’s included:

- A 6’ x 30″ table in the Expo Hall, which is the highest-trafficked part of Disrupt, where more than 10,000 founders, VCs, and tech leaders come looking for partnerships, investments, and networking.
- We provide the table, linen, chairs, and signage so you can focus on showcasing your startup — not logistics.
- 10 Disrupt passes, including five all-access Exhibitor-Partner passes and five Expo+ passes, so your whole team can work the floor, not just staff a booth. Those passes cover nearly $4,000 of the exhibition price by themselves!
- Lead generation through the Disrupt 2026 mobile app, so every conversation turns into a follow-up, not a lost business card.
- Access to the Disrupt 2026 press list and Silver Tier sponsor branding across the site, app, and signage.

Joining fellow exhibitors is the fastest, easiest way for a startup at any stage to get in front of the exact room that matters, from investors sourcing deals, to operators evaluating tools, to founders scouting partners.

There is one catch, though:** Tables are limited.** This offer ends when the last exhibit table sells out, or on September 25 at 11:59 p.m. PT, whichever comes first.

**Book your exhibit table now**. Or, if you’re not ready to exhibit this year, get a Disrupt 2026 ticket while we’re running a limited-time $300 discount and start planning your presence for next year!
