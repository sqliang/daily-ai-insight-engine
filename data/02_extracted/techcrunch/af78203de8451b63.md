- We provide the table, linen, chairs, and signage so you can focus on showcasing your startup — not logistics.

- 10 Disrupt passes, including five all-access Exhibitor-Partner passes and five Expo+ passes, so your whole team can work the floor, not just staff a booth. Those cover nearly $4,000 of the exhibition price by themselves!

- Lead generation through the Disrupt 2026 mobile app, so every conversation turns into a follow-up, not a lost business card.

- Access to the Disrupt 2026 press list and Silver Tier sponsor branding across the site, app, and signage.

Joining fellow exhibitors is the fastest, lowest-lift way for a startup at any stage to get in front of the exact room that matters, from investors sourcing deals, to operators evaluating tools, to founders scouting partners.

There is one catch, though:** Tables are limited.** This offer ends when the last exhibit table sells out, or on September 25 at 11:59 p.m. PT, whichever comes first.
