### 👩💻 AI releases

**Anthropic**news:**Claude Code Desktop**now has a built-in browser so it can read docs, navigate sites, and interact with web pages directly.**Claude for Teachers**gives verified K-12 educators in the US free access to premium features and a built-in teaching skills library.**Cowork**is now available on web and mobile, so your background tasks can run on their own without your device needing to be on.**Reflect**feature helps you track and visualize your Claude usage so you can better understand your habits.

**Canva**launched**Code 2.0**, an upgrade to its AI website builder with faster code generation, HTML import, and drag-and-drop editing.**Dropbox**integrated with**ChatGPT**and can now help you organize files and conduct multi-step workflows directly inside the app.**Google**news:**AI Mode**in**Search**now connects to apps like Canva, Instacart, and YouTube Music, so you can finish tasks directly from Search.**Gemini in Chrome**rolled out to UK desktop users to help summarize pages, conduct comparisons, and integrate with apps like Calendar, Gmail, and more.**Google Images**now features an AI-powered gallery and image generation in Search to mark its 25th anniversary.**Google Photos Video Remix**can create shareable video clips with cinematic relighting, background swapping, and other effects.**Google Vids**added Gemini Omni editing and personal AI avatars that let you put yourself into video clips.**NotebookLM**is now called**Gemini Notebook**and features native code execution and deeper integration with the Gemini ecosystem.**Waze**now includes a motorcycle mode with AI-powered hazard detection plus Gemini conversational search for finding destinations.

**Meta AI**launched**Muse Image**, an image generator that can edit, restore, and restyle your personal photos using AI.**Moonshot AI**open-sourced**Kimi K3**, a multimodal model that gets close to the performance of top-tier frontier models.**OpenAI**news:**ChatGPT Work**connects to Gmail, Google Drive, Salesforce, Slack, and Teams to manage files and run multi-step workflows on your behalf.**GPT-5.6**is a three-tier model family (Sol, Terra, and Luna) with improved intelligence, speed, and agentic capabilities.**GPT-Live**voice models let ChatGPT listen and speak at the same time, making voice conversations feel natural with real-time acknowledgments.
