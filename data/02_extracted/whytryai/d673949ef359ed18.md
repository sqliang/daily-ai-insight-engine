## 👩💻 AI releases

**Adobe**expanded its**Creative Agent**across Firefly and Creative Cloud apps, letting creators describe an outcome and have the agent handle the busywork.**Anthropic**news:**Claude Design**was upgraded so that it stays on-brand, syncs with Claude Code, and can export results to Adobe, Canva, PDF, and PowerPoint.**Claude Code**now supports**Artifacts**that turn your session work into live, self-updating web pages that you can share with your team.

**ByteDance**launched**Seedance 2.0 Mini**, a faster, lower-quality version of its flagship AI video model that also costs half as much per generation.**Character.AI**launched a**Creator Bundle**with new tools to help you build characters faster, track performance, and get discovered via leaderboards.**HappyOyster**launched**HappyOyster 1.0**, an open-ended model for creating and exploring real-time interactive video worlds with prompts. (Try for free.)**Meta**added**AI Mode**to Facebook search, along with new photo and video editing presets that let you swap looks, outfits, and styles.**Microsoft**officially released**Copilot Cowork**, an AI teammate that can send emails, draft documents, manage your calendar, and browse the web.**Midjourney**rolled out**Draft Mode**for V8.1, which lets you quickly generate 24 low-resolution image options before rendering your favorite in full resolution.**OpenAI**news:**ChatGPT**now gives free users more accurate and complete health answers using GPT-5.5 Instant.**Record & Replay**lets you demonstrate your work steps to Codex and turn them into editable, reusable skills to share with others.**Scheduled tasks**now have a dedicated pane in ChatGPT, making reminders and recurring prompts easier to create and manage.

**Perplexity**launched**Brain**for Computer, a memory system that learns from completed tasks to make the agent more precise and work better over time.**Sakana AI**launched**Marlin**, an autonomous research agent that can think for up to eight hours straight to produce full reports and slide decks.**xAI**released**Grok Imagine Video 1.5**, an image-to-video model with better motion consistency and faster generation speed.**Z.ai**open-sourced**GLM-5.2**, an agentic model with a one-million-token context window, built for complex coding and long-horizon tasks. (Try it for free.)
