Nowadays, if you want an AI-generated logo, you can ask just about any image-capable chatbot like ChatGPT or Gemini to make one.

But for this Live & Learn, I wanted to test tools that either position themselves as dedicated AI logo makers or at least offer specialized templates/features for logos.

I narrowed the field to a mix of design tools with AI capabilities recently added, AI image tools with standalone logo templates, and an AI-first logo maker.

I gave all five the same prompt and scored them across the same five dimensions to keep the comparison as fair as possible.
