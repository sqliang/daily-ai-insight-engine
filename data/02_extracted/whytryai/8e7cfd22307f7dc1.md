### 👩💻 AI releases

**Anthropic**news:**Claude Fable 5**has been redeployed after the government lifted its export restrictions, with an improved safety classifier to block harmful requests.**Claude Sonnet 5**is an upgrade to the company’s mid-range model with better coding, reasoning, and tool-use capabilities.

**Cursor**launched a**native iOS app**in public beta, letting developers build apps and manage coding agents from their phone.**Google**news:**Gemini Omni Flash**and**Nano Banana 2 Lite**are now available to developers in more places to support conversational video and image creation.**Gemini Spark**, the 24/7 AI agent, is now available on macOS to automate desktop tasks, integrating with tools like Canva, Dropbox, and Instacart.**NotebookLM**can now turn your sources into 60-second vertical video overviews with narration. (Ultra and Pro subscribers first. Free users later.)**Personal Intelligence**can now generate personalized images with Nano Banana based on your data from Gmail, Google Photos, and YouTube.**Take Notes for Me**can automatically transcribe Google Meet calls and generate summaries, saving everything to Drive. (Ultra and Pro subscribers.)

**Runway**added Google’s**Gemini Omni Flash**to its platform, so users can generate and edit videos from images, text, and video inputs.**Square**now lets restaurants accept orders directly from ChatGPT and Claude, so customers can browse, order, and pay without leaving the chat.**X**launched an**official MCP server**that lets AI agents like Claude and Cursor fetch timelines, manage bookmarks, and search posts more easily.
