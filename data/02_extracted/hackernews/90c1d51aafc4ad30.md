Someone asked what I'd do if I were 17. I'd learn how to build LLMs from scratch, and then train ones as powerful as I could with whatever hardware I could get access to.

# Paul Graham on X: "Someone asked what I'd do if I were 17. I'd learn how to build LLMs from scratch, and then train ones as powerful as I could with whatever hardware I could get access to."

- Notice that what I would not do is try to start a startup. Instead I'd build the foundation of knowledge to base a startup on later. Way better startup ideas would grow out of understanding LLMs deeply than trying to start companies based on what I knew at 17.
