`Space`/ tap — switch lanes

`Esc`— title

**DONKEY.BAS** shipped with early IBM PC DOS as a demo of color graphics and sound in BASICA.
It was written by Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates and Neil Konzen in 1981 (version 1.10 in 1982).
You only switch lanes — avoid the donkey, or BOOM.

This page recreates the original CGA gameplay in JavaScript with some liberties. Original source: DONKEY.BAS · GitHub
