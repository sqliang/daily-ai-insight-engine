By contrast, these data suggest that external stimuli, such as reward, are interpreted in the light of momentary internal bodily state, in this case cardiac vagal tone, which is a crucial factor that needs to be considered.

31Importantly, compared with other slow-breathing techniques, prolonged exhalation is particularly easy to learn because there is no need to fully standardize the breathing rate, allowing participants to breathe at their own pace.
