**The Rundown:** 200+ AI researchers and economists, including 16 Nobel winners, just [signed](https://www.wemustactnow.ai/?utm_campaign=economists-researchers-put-ai-s-job-shock-on-the-clock&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) "We Must Act Now," a Stanford-organized statement telling governments to build safety nets and labor policy for an economic shift like the Industrial Revolution.

**The details:**

*   The three core points: AI will grow "radically more powerful" within 10 years, the shift could be the biggest and fastest ever, and prep must start now.

*   Several leaders from top AI labs signed, including Google's Jeff Dean, Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark, and OpenAI's Noam Brown.

*   UVA Economist Anton Korinek [said](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/wemustactnow/?utm_campaign=economists-researchers-put-ai-s-job-shock-on-the-clock&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai), "Steam, electricity, and computers each gave societies decades to adapt; AI may give us only a few years."
