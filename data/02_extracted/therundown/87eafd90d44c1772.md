**The Rundown:**Black Forest Labs just [opened](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3?utm_campaign=black-forest-labs-teaches-video-ai-to-run-robots&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) early access to FLUX 3, a ‘visual intelligence’ AI that generates video, images, and synced audio, with the German lab also spinning it into FLUX-mimic, a robot-control variant for Audi's production lines.

**The details:**

*   FLUX 3 produces 20-second clips with native audio, with capabilities including multilingual dialogue, typography, and style/character adherence.

*   The model can handle text, image, and video inputs, with BFL's testing showing its outputs favored over rivals like Runway, Kling, and Grok Imagine.

*   The full rollout will include video, image, and partner-specific action models, plus an open-weight FLUX 3 Dev sized to fit on factory hardware.

*   FLUX-mimic, [built](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-mimic?utm_campaign=black-forest-labs-teaches-video-ai-to-run-robots&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) with Zurich-based mimic robotics, learns a new factory task from about a half-hour of demonstration data instead of the usual 30+ hours.
