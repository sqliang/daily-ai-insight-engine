**The Rundown:**Anthropic just [released](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5?utm_campaign=anthropic-s-opus-5-surprise&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) Claude Opus 5 across the Claude apps, Claude Code, and the API, describing it as a “thoughtful and proactive” AI that nears the intelligence of its Fable 5 model at half the price.

**The details:**

*   Opus 5 hits SOTA on agentic terminal coding, knowledge work, agentic search, and computer use tasks, outperforming both Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.

*   On ARC-AGI-3, it [hit](https://x.com/arcprize/status/2080716561539907928?s=20&utm_campaign=anthropic-s-opus-5-surprise&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai) 30.2%, 3x as high as the next best model, also [ranking](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-5-6-sol?utm_campaign=anthropic-s-opus-5-surprise&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=www.therundown.ai#intelligence-tabs) #1 on Artificial Analysis’ Intelligence Index leaderboard.

*   Anthropic ran the new model on International Math Olympiad 2026 problems, where it scored 42 out of 42, well past the 29-point gold threshold.
