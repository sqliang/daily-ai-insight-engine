###### Z AI

**The Rundown: **Chinese AI lab Z AI just confirmed that Ox Alpha, the anonymous free model that took over the internet, is its new GLM-5.3-Flash model — with the company publishing the weights and setting the price at a fraction of the cost of frontier rivals.

Ox Alpha claimed OpenRouter’s No.1 slot with numbers that doubled the second-place DeepSeek, a debut the platform ranked as its biggest ever.

Z AI said the free usage week ran entirely on Chinese-made chips, claiming the setup now serves tokens about as cheaply as Nvidia hardware.
