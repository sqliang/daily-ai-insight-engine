#### 🌎 **[Moonshot publishes weights for powerful Kimi K3](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3)**

![Image 2](https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,quality=80,format=auto,onerror=redirect/uploads/asset/file/db0224fe-8400-4b60-bdb1-6f5d5c5bfc80/k3-open.png)
Image source: Moonshot AI

**The Rundown:**Moonshot AI just officially [released](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3) the weights and tech report behind its powerful Kimi K3, letting anyone (with serious GPUs) download and run the 2.8T-parameter model, the largest ever put out with open weights.

**The details:**

*   K3 was released earlier this month, making waves after competing with both Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6-Sol across a variety of benchmarks.

*   Free weights don’t mean free to run, with the large model requiring much heavier GPUs than the average consumer hardware to run locally.

*   Moonshot also opened core pieces of its stack, like attention kernels and agent infrastructure, under a license letting anyone host and sell K3 access.

*   U.S. officials [accused](https://www.therundown.ai/p/openai-cyber-test-escapes-the-lab) Moonshot of distilling U.S. models (copying their outputs) to build K3, which Beijing [rejected](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-accuses-us-ai-hegemonism-threatens-countermeasures-over-potential-probes-2026-07-27/) as "typical acts of AI hegemony."
