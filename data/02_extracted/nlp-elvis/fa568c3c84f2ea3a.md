### Moonshot Open-Sources Kimi K3

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8T-parameter open-weight MoE model with native vision that lands closer to the closed frontier than any prior open release.

**Architecture:**Combines Kimi Delta Attention, Attention Residuals, and Stable LatentMoE, activating 16 of 896 routed experts and 104B parameters per token.**Scale and context:**Ships a 1-million-token context window and roughly 2.5x better scaling efficiency than Kimi K2.**Agentic post-training:**Uses million-token agentic RL with persistent rollout and sandbox state, plus multiple reasoning-effort levels for long-horizon execution.**Where it lands:**Frontier-level on long-horizon coding, agentic, reasoning, and vision tasks, trailing only Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol among models evaluated.
