## Top Stories

### Anthropic Launches Claude Sonnet 5

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5, its most agentic Sonnet yet, built to plan, use tools, and run autonomously at a level that recently required larger, more expensive models.

**Agentic core:**Sonnet 5 makes plans, drives browsers and terminals, and completes complex tasks where previous Sonnets stopped short, checking its own output without being asked.**Near-Opus at lower cost:**Anthropic says performance is close to Opus 4.8 on reasoning, tool use, coding, and knowledge work, at a lower price point.**Broad availability:**Now the default on Free and Pro and available to Max, Team, and Enterprise, live across all Claude apps and the Claude Platform.**Intro pricing:**Ships with introductory pricing through August, aimed at high-volume agentic workloads.

### xAI Launches Grok Voice Agent Builder

xAI introduced Voice Agent Builder, a no-code platform for creating human-like voice agents powered by Grok Voice.

**No-code creation:**Build production voice agents without writing code, configuring behavior and voice through the builder.**Grok Voice:**Agents run on Grok Voice for low-latency, natural-sounding speech aimed at real-time conversational use cases.**Usage pricing:**Available today at 0.05 dollars per minute, positioning it for customer support, sales, and other high-volume voice workflows.**Ecosystem play:**Extends xAI’s push into agentic products alongside its hosted X MCP and Grok API tooling.
