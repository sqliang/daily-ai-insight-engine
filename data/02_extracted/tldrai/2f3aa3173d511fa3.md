## About

See how CoderPad’s AI Interview Designer transforms a job description into role-relevant, hard-to-game interview questions that deliver reliable, high-quality candidate signal in minutes. See what it’s like to move beyond static question banks with an endless supply of fresh, role-specific interview questions.

**Today’s Hiring Challenges Need Better Questions**

*Many hiring teams are facing challenges like:*

- Generic or recycled interview questions that don’t measure real AI collaboration
- Leaked or overused prompts that candidates can prepare for in advance
- Wasted time creating role-specific interview questions
- Assessments that lag behind rapidly changing job requirements

## Why Attend?

- AI Interview Designer enables anyone on your team to create custom, realistic interviews that reveal how candidates interact with and use AI.
- Whether you’re hiring Software Engineers, Data Engineers, Full-Stack Developers, Front-End Developers, or non-technical talent, you’ll leave with a repeatable framework for building better interviews.
- Join to learn how you can create one free Project or Multi-file Interview for your team.
