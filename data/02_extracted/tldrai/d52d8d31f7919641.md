### LIVE SHOWCASE: July 8th, 10am ET

**PII Detection, Service Accounts, RBAC, and What's Next for Production AI**

Enterprise AI projects don't stall at the model layer; they stall when governance breaks down at scale. When sensitive data crosses boundaries unchecked, when agents mask human credentials, and when no one can answer a compliance team's questions about what ran, when, and why.

Join us for a live walkthrough of Connect AI’s new, HIPAA-compliant feature set: every new capability, live in the product, with real workflows and direct Q&A with the product team.

### What you’ll learn

The showcase covers five new capabilities, demoed live in the product:

**HIPAA-compliant architecture** – Connect AI can now connect EHRs, payer systems, analytics platforms, and enterprise applications with the governed architecture, documented ePHI flows, and audit trails that compliance requires.

**PII detection and token masking** — Enforce configurable warn, redact, or block policies at the MCP tool-call boundary, inbound and outbound, before sensitive data reaches an AI model or returns to a user.

**Service accounts for agents** — Give every autonomous process its own scoped, auditable machine identity. Agents and scheduled pipelines connect without requiring a human OAuth session, and every action they take is independently traceable.

**Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** — Four universal platform roles plus custom role definitions with additive permissions, scoped to specific connections and workspaces. A second governance layer on top of source-inherited permissions.

**Connect AI skills** — Downloadable plugins, published on GitHub, that guide Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other LLMs through the correct source discovery, reducing token overhead and eliminating first-call failures.
