# Claude in Chrome

A helping hand across all your tabs

Claude in Chrome reads the page you’re signed in to, then clicks, types, and fills forms while you decide what happens next.

A refreshing way to work on the web

### From browser research to finished deliverables

### Works with Claude Code

Connect development workflows to Chrome for a partner that tests and iterates with you.

### Uses your everyday tools

Bring Claude into your workflow to make apps work better, without leaving the browser.

### Runs tasks in the background

Start a workflow and move on. Claude completes tasks while you focus elsewhere.

### Pull metrics from analytics dashboards

Claude can navigate your analytics dashboard, extract the numbers you need, and compile them into a summary. No exports, no tab-switching, no manual copying.

### Organize files in Google Drive

Claude can sort through your Drive, create a folder structure, move files where they belong, and flag duplicates and old files for you to review. You approve the changes instead of doing the sorting yourself.

### Prepare and plan from your calendar

Claude can read your calendar, pull context from email threads, flag which meetings you need to prepare for, and book rooms if they’re missing. Run it every evening and start each day knowing what's coming.

### Research competitors, get a comparison deck

Claude in Chrome visits competitor sites and pulls pricing, features, and positioning. Claude Cowork compiles everything into a formatted PowerPoint ready for your next strategy meeting.

### Log sales calls to your CRM

Claude can read your calendar, match attendees to Salesforce contacts, and draft activity logs for each call. You add notes and approve before anything gets created.

### Clean up promotional emails

Claude can scan through your inbox, identify marketing emails, newsletters, and automated notifications, then present them as a list for you to review before deleting in bulk.

Reach the web that has no API

### Your session follows you

Every conversation saves to your history. Sessions live with your account, not the machine, so you can start in Chrome and pick it up on desktop or mobile without needing to re-explain. A skill you save in the panel runs from any surface too.

Three ways to access the extension

## Stay in control

Hidden instructions on a page can try to redirect Claude’s actions. Though Claude in Chrome has built-in defenses that have been tested against real attacks, we recommend the habits below as a best practice.

### What we built

#### Automatically approve actions with guardrails

If you choose to, Claude can work through a task without asking you to approve each step. Before it acts on a page, a separate safety check reviews the action for risk and for instructions hidden by the site. It will surface to you if anything seems off.

#### Give permissions, site by site

If you prefer more control, switch to Permissions Mode. You grant access one site at a time. Claude can't reach anything you haven't approved.

### Claude stops before taking sensitive actions

Purchases, financial actions, and other one-way doors wait for you.

### Admins decide for the organization

On Team and Enterprise, admins turn the extension on or off org-wide and set site allowlists and blocklists.

### What we recommend

### Don’t use on sensitive workflows

Skip banking, health records, and anything with credentials you wouldn't share. Start with workflows you already know well.

## FAQ

Claude Cowork users can start a task in Cowork and let it handle work in the browser without switching windows by enabling Claude in Chrome as a connector.

Avoid financial transactions, password management, or anything involving sensitive personal data. Start with trusted sites and familiar workflows where you're comfortable having Claude take actions. Make sure your use complies with our acceptable use policy. Learn more.

Browser AI faces unique security risks, like prompt injection attacks, where malicious actors might try to trick Claude into unintended actions, such as sharing your bank information or deleting important files. While we’ve implemented protections, they aren’t foolproof. Attack vectors are constantly evolving and Claude may hallucinate, leading to actions that you did not intend. We’ve shared our testing results, including possible attack scenarios, so you can make informed decisions, and strongly encourage you to read about the risks before using this product. Read blog post.
