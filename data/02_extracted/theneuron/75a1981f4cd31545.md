**How do I actually use AI agents to save time in my business?**

In five minutes, we are going live with [**James McAulay**](https://agentaccelerator.ai/?utm_source=www.theneurondaily.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=livestream-how-to-build-agents-for-total-beginners), founder of The Agent Accelerator, for a practical crash course on everything you need to know to get started **building helpful, proactive agents in Claude Cowork and Claude Code.**

By month five, it recorded its first $200K-plus month. He did it without a single employee or a dollar of paid advertising. Instead, James delegates work to multiple AI agents every day.

*   **Agent foundations:** How to move from prompting a chatbot to delegating agentic work.

*   **Your questions:** Answered live!
